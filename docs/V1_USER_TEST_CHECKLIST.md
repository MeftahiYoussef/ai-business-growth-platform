## Journey

1. Register or log in.
2. Complete business setup.
3. Review the dashboard.
4. Open Profile and edit at least one business field.
5. Generate content ideas.
6. Save one idea.
7. Copy one idea.
8. Delete one unsaved idea.
9. Open AI Studio.
10. Generate a seven-day plan.
11. Review all seven items.
12. Convert selected items into drafts.
13. Edit and save one draft.
14. Open the calendar/list and verify the draft appears.
15. Generate a report.
16. Confirm an alert/notification appears.
17. Download JSON export.
18. Download CSV export.
19. Log out.
20. Log in again and verify data is still present.

## Known Demo Limitations

- The v1 MVP is single-business from the user-facing interface.
- Render Free can sleep after inactivity, causing cold-start delay.
- Competitors, advanced trend intelligence, automatic publishing, billing, and knowledge base/RAG are post-v1.
